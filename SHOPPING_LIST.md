# Shopping List - Woodshop Build

Checkable, store-by-store shopping list. Pricing is a 2026 estimate;
verify at your local store. Check off items as you load the truck.

> **Before you shop:** confirm the planer-lift mechanism choice
> (scissor stabilizer vs gas struts). The default list below assumes
> the scissor stabilizer + drill design.

---

## Trip 1: Home Depot

### Lumber department

- [ ] 2x4 x 8 ft SPF stud - **26 boards** ($3 ea, $78). Reject any with
      visible twist, crown, or large knots. Pull from the middle of the
      stack.
- [ ] 2x4 x 12 ft SPF - **2 boards** ($7 ea, $14). For the 144 in front
      and back top rails of the top leg. (Or splice with a stud at a
      cross-stretcher, but one piece is cleaner.)

### Sheet goods

- [ ] 3/4 in birch or sande plywood 4 x 8 sheet - **6 sheets** ($60-80
      ea birch, $40-50 sande). Inspect for delamination at corners.
      Reject anything with internal voids you can see at the edge.
- [ ] OPTIONAL: 3/4 in MDF 4 x 8 sheet - **1 sheet** ($35) for a
      sacrificial bench-top layer.

### Fasteners aisle

- [ ] #9 x 2.5 in construction screws - **1 lb box, ~250 ct** (~$35).
      GRK R4 or SPAX. These are your primary frame fastener.
- [ ] #8 x 1.25 in wood screws - **1 lb box** (~$10). For toe kicks,
      panels, anything thinner than 1.5 in.
- [ ] 1/4 x 3.5 in lag screws - **8 ct** ($5). For anchoring frame to
      wall studs.
- [ ] 1/4 in flat washers - **8 ct** ($1).
- [ ] 3/8 x 4 in lag screws - **4 ct** ($4). For tying the Husky chests
      to the bench frame so the bench doesn't shift.

### Adhesives + finish

- [ ] Loctite PL Premium construction adhesive - **2 tubes** ($14).
- [ ] Titebond II or III wood glue - **1 quart** ($13).
- [ ] Boiled linseed oil OR General Finishes Arm-R-Seal - **1 quart**
      ($20).
- [ ] Mineral spirits - **1 quart** ($10).
- [ ] Foam brushes 5-pack - **1 pack** ($5).
- [ ] Lint-free shop rags - **1 bag** ($5).

### Plumbing aisle (dust collection)

- [ ] 4 in S&D PVC pipe, 10 ft - **3 lengths** ($12 ea, $36).
- [ ] 4 in 45-degree wye PVC fitting - **4 ct** ($14 ea, $56).
- [ ] 4 in 45-degree elbow PVC - **4 ct** ($9 ea, $36).
- [ ] 4 in long-radius 90 elbow PVC - **2 ct** ($13 ea, $26).
- [ ] 4 in to 2.5 in PVC reducer - **2 ct** ($9 ea, $18).
- [ ] PVC pipe hangers (10 ct pack) - **2 packs** ($10 ea, $20).
- [ ] Foil HVAC tape (50 ft roll) - **1 roll** ($12).

### Electrical aisle (if DIYing rough-in - otherwise the electrician
brings these)

- [ ] Bare copper grounding wire 14 ga - **25 ft spool** ($20).

### Tools to verify / buy (skip if you already own)

- [ ] Self-centering hinge bit set (for cabinet hardware): $15
- [ ] 3 in hole saw (for dust-collection ports through plywood): $20
- [ ] Forstner bit set if doing flush router insert: $35
- [ ] Long level, 4 ft minimum: $25 (worth owning permanently)

**Home Depot subtotal: $475-650 depending on plywood grade.**

## Trip 2: Harbor Freight

- [ ] HAUL-MASTER 2.5 Ton Trailer/RV Scissor Stabilizer (SKU #96406)
      - **1** ($38). The lift mechanism. **Open the box in-store and
      measure the input shaft hex size** - you'll need a matching
      socket for the drill adapter.
- [ ] 4 in aluminum blast gate - **4 ct** ($14 ea, $56). Cheaper than
      Home Depot's. Check that the slide moves freely before paying.
- [ ] 4 in PVC flex hose - **2 x 6 ft** ($20 ea, $40).
- [ ] 4 in hose clamps - **8 ct** ($3 ea, $24).
- [ ] OPTIONAL: spare 4 in coupler kit - **1** ($10) for repair stock.

**Harbor Freight subtotal: ~$170.**

## Trip 3: Amazon or local source

### Hardware for the planer lift mechanism

- [ ] 1/4 in hex to 3/4 in socket adapter - **1** ($5). Verify your
      jack's input is 3/4 in - some are 13/16 in.
- [ ] Magnetic reed switch with magnet - **2** ($10 ea, $20). For
      top-of-travel and bottom-of-travel cutoffs.
- [ ] 12V relay rated 10 A - **1** ($8).
- [ ] 1/2 in steel hitch pin 6 in long - **1** ($4).
- [ ] 1/2 in pipe coupling (used as pin receiver) - **2** ($3 ea, $6).
- [ ] 2 in x 2 in x 1/8 in steel angle, 12 in length - **1** ($5).

### Tool integration

- [ ] Router insert plate, 11.75 x 9.25 phenolic with leveling screws
      - **1** ($80). Rockler, Woodpeckers, or MLCS - all comparable.
- [ ] Router insert ring set (4-6 rings, fits the above) - **1 set**
      ($30).
- [ ] OPTIONAL: 4 ft T-track for miter-saw stops - **2** ($20 ea, $40).
- [ ] OPTIONAL: 3/4 in HDPE leveling pucks or cabinet feet - **6**
      ($15 set).

### Drill mounting (improvise if you have scraps)

- [ ] Hose clamps 2 in - **2** ($3 ea, $6) for cradling the drill body.
- [ ] Zip ties, 8 in heavy duty - **1 pack** ($5) for holding the
      trigger and tidying wiring.

**Amazon / online subtotal: ~$200.**

## Trip 4: Discuss with your electrician

Don't buy this stuff yourself unless you're licensed. Take the
electrician through the bay first; let them quote materials and labor.
The list below is just so you know what to expect.

- [ ] Walk the bay with the electrician
- [ ] Discuss circuit count and amp ratings (see `build_sequence.md`
      Phase 2)
- [ ] Discuss PVC vs metal dust trunk - **don't commit until they
      weigh in on static bonding**
- [ ] Get a written quote

Typical electrician scope:
- 4-5 dedicated 20 A circuits + breakers
- Optional dedicated 240 V circuit for planer
- Several hundred feet of 12/2 Romex
- 8-10 wall outlet boxes at z = 44 in
- 1 ceiling outlet
- Light switches
- Per local code: GFCI / AFCI as required

**Budget: $400-800 materials, $1,500-3,000 labor.**

## Substitutions and skips

- **Skip sande, use birch** if you want a furniture-grade look. Skip
  the optional MDF top layer if you're not planning to chew up the
  bench in the first year.
- **Skip the gas-strut alternative** entirely if you're going scissor +
  drill. They're documented in `planer_lift_bom.md` as the fallback.
- **Skip the T-track** until you've used the miter saw for a while and
  know whether you want stops.

## Don't skip

- The pin-lock hardware for the planer lift. Non-negotiable.
- The reed switches for the drill cutoff. Non-negotiable.
- Construction adhesive on the bench top doubler. The screws alone
  will let it creak forever.
- 36 in walking clearance during build - if the bench gets in your way
  during assembly, you'll be living with it forever.
